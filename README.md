# Revive

### Connecting blood donors with people in need.

**[Explore the live app](https://revive-blood.vercel.app/)**

Revive is a community-driven blood donation platform for Bangladesh. It connects voluntary donors with people seeking blood and helps organizations coordinate donation drives. Users can search by blood group and location, send requests, and follow donor responses in one place.

## Key Features

- **Find donors:** Search by blood group and district to discover relevant donors.
- **Donor profiles:** Register as a donor and manage profile and donation-related information.
- **Blood requests:** Send requests to selected donors with the hospital, location, required date, and additional details.
- **Response tracking:** Donors can review and respond to requests, while requesters track acceptances.
- **Controlled contact access:** After a donor accepts, requesters confirm contact access through their dashboard to view the donor's email.
- **Donation events:** Browse blood drives by district or division and register to attend.
- **Organization management:** Verified organizations can host donation drives and manage registrations.

## User Roles

| Role | Main capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Donor | Maintain a donor profile, respond to requests, and join events. |
| Requester | Find donors, send blood requests, and track responses. |
| Organization | Organize donation drives and manage participation. |
| Admin | Verify organizations and moderate platform activity. |
